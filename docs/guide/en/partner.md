# Partner guide

This guide is for the **focal point** of a partner organisation, in charge of sending the organisation's data to the project. There are two ways to work: with the application, or with the Excel form only.

## With the application

### Joining the project

1. Install the application (see the [user guide](user-guide.md)).
2. On the **Projects** screen, follow **Join a team project**. Give your name and save your **identity file** (`.smeid`).
3. Send this file to the project's administrator. They will call or write to check with you the **fingerprint** shown under your name: compare the two strings.
4. The administrator sends you back an **invitation** (`.smejoin`) and gives you its password another way.
5. Go back to **Join a team project**, choose the invitation, type the password, then choose where to save your copy. If the project holds sensitive data, also give a password for your copy.

Your copy holds the project's design (results, indicators, calendar, places, disaggregations) and your organisation's data only.

### Entering and sending the data of a period

1. Open the **Reception** screen and choose the period.
2. Open your organisation's submission (or each of your sites', depending on the project) and enter the values with their breakdowns.
3. Correct blocking alerts; justify in writing the review alerts when the value is right.
4. Attach the supporting documents asked for (attendance lists, registers).
5. Click **Submit**.
6. Send your changes the way agreed with the administrator:
   - **package**: Team and security tab of the project sheet, "Prepare a package" for the administrator, then send the file by email;
   - **shared folder** or **server**: "Synchronise now".

When the administrator or validator has handled your submission, its state comes back at the next exchange: **validated**, or **returned** with a comment. A returned submission is corrected and submitted again. A validated submission can no longer be changed on your side: if a correction is needed, ask the administrator.

## Without the application: the Excel form

The administrator can send you an **Excel form** for a period: a protected workbook where only the white cells can be filled.

- One line per indicator and, if it is broken down, per category (women, men, not specified).
- For a percentage or a rate, give the **numerator and the denominator**, never the percentage itself.
- For a milestone, choose "Done" or "Not done" from the list (1 and 0 are also accepted).
- Give the source or reference document at the bottom.
- **Do not move, add or delete lines**: the workbook would be refused on return.

Send the workbook back to the administrator. If an error is found after validation, they will ask for a new workbook; your two versions are compared before the correction is applied.

## What you see and do not see

You see the project's design and your organisation's data. You do not see other partners' data, feedback and complaints, the budget or the project's other registers. Even with access to the team's shared folder or server, the files that do not concern you are encrypted for others and cannot be read by your copy.
